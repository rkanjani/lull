Keep your BedJet 3 up to date without the official app.

• Check for a firmware update from the BedJet sheet. Your BedJet downloads it over its own Wi-Fi and installs it, and Lull tells you when it's back on the new version.
• When your BedJet says newer firmware is available, Lull offers it once the air is off.
• Give your BedJet 3 the Wi-Fi network it uses for updates, by name. Lull remembers the name, never the password, and still never asks for your location.
